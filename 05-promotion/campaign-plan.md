Short vertical videos, countdowns and behind-the-scenes clips are what fills camps today; a poster has to read in seconds and work across feed, story and print with small changes. That is why every format here shares one anatomy.

## Teaser «من البحر… لوين؟» (28 → 30 November)

Files: `04-designs/exports/teaser/` (how it was made: `04-designs/teaser/README.md`). The teaser asks the question and the reveal answers it with the poster's tagline, «من البحر… إلى النخيل». If the poster is already out, the teaser still works as a game: followers who saw it answer fast, and those comments push the Reel to more people.

1. **Day 1, evening** (check the page's Insights for when followers are online):
   - Post `WinterCamp-Teaser-Reel-9x16.mp4` as a Reel, with the cover on the first frame.
   - Tag Mahdia as the location, not Tozeur, which would give the answer away.
   - Share it to the story and the parents' WhatsApp status the same evening.
2. **Same evening, story:** post `WinterCamp-Teaser-Story-Quiz-9x16.jpg` with Instagram's **Quiz** sticker in the empty band under the clues.
   - Question: «وين مخيمنا؟»
   - Options: جربة · **توزر** (correct) · عين دراهم · طبرقة.
3. **First hour, comments:** reply to every guess without giving the answer: «سخون 🔥» when close, «بارد ❄️» when far, «قريب… 😉».
   - Pin one comment: «التلميح الخامس؟ الجواب الإثنين 👀».
   - Quick replies count: Instagram looks at how fast comments and sends come in.
4. **Day 2:** the carousel (`Carousel-1…6`) in the feed for people who prefer to swipe. Slide 1 also works alone as a single post.
5. **Day 3, evening:**
   - Post `WinterCamp-Reveal-Reel-9x16.mp4`, tagging Degache or Tozeur as the location.
   - In story, show the quiz result («كم واحد عرفها؟») and thank the first person who got it right.
   - Then go back to the normal plan (FAQ, namesakes, countdown).

The Reels have their own original sound, so they work even on a business account without music. To use a trending Tunisian song instead, post the `-silent` file and pick the song in Instagram.

## Djerid reference sheet (for posts and reels)

- **Architecture**: Ouled el-Hadef's brick facades in Tozeur; patterns cast shade that cools the walls; bricks fired with palm fronds.
- **Food**: Deglet Nour dates («دقلة النور», "dates of light"), mtabga (Berber flat pie from the south), Djerid chickpea harissa, couscous; dates stuffed with almonds.
- **Crafts**: palm-leaf weaving (baskets, mats, hats), wool kilims, palm wood, pottery jars (like the ones on the camp's well), the Tozeur «مقردش» striped jebba of silk and wool.
- **Nature and animals**: the oasis palm groves, Chott el-Djerid (salt flats, mirages, flamingo flocks), fennec fox, dromedary, dorcas gazelle and houbara bustard in the region's protected areas.
- **Events**: the International Oasis Festival in Tozeur happens every late December (after the camp): good for a «come back with your family» post.

- [ ] Same poster in every parents' WhatsApp group; story version as WhatsApp status the same evening.
- [ ] A4 poster printed and put up at the group's headquarters and partner schools (`WinterCamp-Degache-Print-A4.pdf`, print without scaling).
- [ ] Countdown story each day from 5 December: `04-designs/exports/countdown/WinterCamp-Countdown-7.jpg` → `-1.jpg` (the last two show 48 and 24 hours).
- [ ] Every caption ends with «للتسجيل والاستفسار: 22 882 867» (not the teaser's, which keeps the secret until the reveal).
- [ ] Teaser Reel + quiz story posted; every guess answered within the first hour; reveal two days later.
- [ ] Before posting any camp photo or video: no recognisable child without consent (see `02-photos/README.md`).

## Namesake posts (2–4 December)

Two carousels of 4–5 slides, same identity (night ground, the unit badge on the first slide):

- «من هو عبد المجيد عطية؟» — born in Mahdia in 1925, teacher, writer of the novel «المنبت» and of children's stories such as «نجمة البحر الصغير». Why the scouts unit carries his name, and why there is a little starfish by the lighthouse on the poster.
- «من هي بشيرة بن مراد؟» — 1913–1993, founded the Muslim Union of Tunisian Women in 1936, fought for girls' education. Why the guides carry her name.
