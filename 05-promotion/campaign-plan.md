Short vertical videos, countdowns and behind-the-scenes clips are what fills camps today; a poster has to read in seconds and work across feed, story and print with small changes. That is why every format here shares one anatomy.

## Djerid reference sheet (for posts and reels)

- **Architecture**: Ouled el-Hadef's brick facades in Tozeur; patterns cast shade that cools the walls; bricks fired with palm fronds.
- **Food**: Deglet Nour dates («دقلة النور», "dates of light"), mtabga (Berber flat pie from the south), Djerid chickpea harissa, couscous; dates stuffed with almonds.
- **Crafts**: palm-leaf weaving (baskets, mats, hats), wool kilims, palm wood, pottery jars (like the ones on the camp's well), the Tozeur «مقردش» striped jebba of silk and wool.
- **Nature and animals**: the oasis palm groves, Chott el-Djerid (salt flats, mirages, flamingo flocks), fennec fox, dromedary, dorcas gazelle and houbara bustard in the region's protected areas.
- **Events**: the International Oasis Festival in Tozeur happens every late December (after the camp): good for a «come back with your family» post.

## Posting checklist

- [ ] Launch post pinned on the Facebook page, with the poster as the first image and the phone number in the first line of the caption.
- [ ] Same poster in every parents' WhatsApp group; story version as WhatsApp status the same evening.
- [ ] A4 poster printed and put up at the group's headquarters and partner schools (`WinterCamp-Degache-Print-A4.pdf`, print without scaling).
- [ ] Countdown story each day from 5 December: `04-designs/exports/countdown/WinterCamp-Countdown-7.jpg` → `-1.jpg` (the last two show 48 and 24 hours).
- [ ] Every caption ends with «للتسجيل والاستفسار: 22 882 867».
- [ ] Before posting any camp photo or video: no recognisable child without consent (see `02-photos/README.md`).
