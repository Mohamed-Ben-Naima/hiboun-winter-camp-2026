# 01 · Group profile — who is telling the story

Research notes gathered on 24 November 2026 to write the posters in the group's own voice. Sources at the end.

## Les Scouts Tunisiens — الكشافة التونسية

- National scouting organisation of Tunisia, coeducational. Founded in 1933–34 (sources give both years; the movement celebrated its 90th anniversary in 2023). Member of the World Organization of the Scout Movement since 1957 and a full member of the World Association of Girl Guides and Girl Scouts. The guides are an integral but independent section.
- Motto: «كن مستعدا» — Be prepared. Recognised as being of public utility in 1977. Voluntary, independent, educational, open to all.
- Known for citizenship training through community service: tree planting, building, literacy, support to orphanages, disaster relief.
- Structure: national commissariat in Tunis led by the national commissioner (القائد العام) → regions (24) with their regional council (المجلس الجهوي) → about 350 groups (أفواج) → units by age and gender.
- Age sections: cubs (أشبال) and brownies (زهرات) 7–12 · **scouts (كشافة) and guides (مرشدات) 12–17** · rovers (جوالة) 17–23. The winter camp (12–17) is exactly the scouts-and-guides section.
- Units are usually named after a figure: «فرقة <name> للكشافة», for example.
- Promise and law (common to Arab-region scouting): trustworthy, loyal, useful and helpful, a friend to all and a brother or sister to every scout, courteous, a friend to animals and plants, obedient, keeps their spirit in difficulty, thrifty, clean in thought, word and deed.

## Fawj Hiboun — فوج هيبون (Mahdia region)

- Hiboun is a town and commune at the north-western entrance of Mahdia; a farming village (olives, fruit, vegetables) until the early 1970s, now part of the Mahdia tourist area. Mahdia: former Fatimid capital, Skifa el-Kahla gate, Borj el-Kebir, the Cap Afrique lighthouse (1890) at the tip of the peninsula, Tunisia's largest fishing port.
- The group's two units for 12–17 year-olds:
  - **فرقة عبد المجيد عطية للكشافة** (Abdelmajid Attia Scout Unit). Abdelmajid Attia (born in Mahdia, 13 March 1925 – died 15 May 2014, buried in Mahdia): writer, primary then secondary-school teacher, author of the novel «المنبت» (1967) and of children's stories such as «نجمة البحر الصغير» (1979) and «عمي الدحداح بياع التفاح».
  - **مرشدات بشيرة بن مراد** (Bchira Ben Mrad Guides). Bchira Ben Mrad (1913–1993): pioneer of the Tunisian women's movement, founded and chaired the Muslim Union of Tunisian Women (1936–1956), campaigned for girls' education and for the national cause.
- Online presence: no public Facebook, Instagram or website for the group shows up in search engines (Facebook pages are often not indexed). The only public trace found is a 2020 Mahdia municipal council record that lists the group's leader. Opportunity: one consistent page with this identity, and the namesake posts in `05-promotion/`.

## What this means for the posters

- Speak to both units: always «كشافة ومرشدات», «فتية وفتيات».
- The units carry the names of a Mahdia storyteller and a pioneer woman: hence the storybook voice («كان يا مكان»), the chapters, and the small starfish by the lighthouse.
- The camp is a journey from the sea (Mahdia) to the palms (Degache, on the northern shore of Chott el-Djerid, 12 km east of Tozeur, an oasis farmed since Roman times and known for its Deglet Nour dates).
- Values to show: adventure with responsibility, friendship, service, respect for nature and for the places we visit.
