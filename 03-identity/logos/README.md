Rules

- Order in a row, right to left: scouts unit · Fawj Hiboun seal · guides unit. The seal is the largest (about 1.2× the unit badges' height).
- Never recolour, stretch, crop or redraw the badges, and never add effects other than the thin cream ring around the seal used on the posters.
- Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the seal's diameter around the row.
- The fleur-de-lis seal belongs to the Tunisian Scouts and the group: use it only for the group's own activities.
