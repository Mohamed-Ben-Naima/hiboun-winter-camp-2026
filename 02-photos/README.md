# 02 · Photos

Photos of the campsite (Camping / Espace Badi, Degache) received on WhatsApp on 21 November 2026, renamed by what they show and sorted by use. The original WhatsApp file names are kept in the tables so nothing is lost.

- `graded/` — the five photos prepared for the designs (upscaled, warm grade, fine grain). Rebuild them with `04-designs/source/grade_photos.py`.
- `rejected/` — not usable: a desert-quad photo that is not Tozeur, and a low-quality screenshot.
- `private/` — photos where people, including children, can be recognised. The folder is in `.gitignore`: it stays in the local folder and is never pushed to GitHub.

Rule: no identifiable faces of minors in anything we publish without that child's photo consent. Prefer backs, hands, silhouettes, tents and landscapes. New camp photos of scouts go in `private/` until consent is collected.
